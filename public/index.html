No fine print

What works offline, and what uses the internet.

Most GPS apps hide basic features behind logins or subscriptions. Here is exactly how TrackMe works in plain English.

Works 100% offline — no account needed

Recording drives, rides, walks, and runs. Live speedometer, floating mini-display over Maps, hands-free Auto-Stop & Auto-Start (Continuous Trip Tracking), ride history, speed & elevation charts, GPX import/export, and route image/video sharing all work completely offline and signed out.

Uses a free account & internet connection

1-tap Live Location Sharing, Trusted Contact alerts, Group Rides, and optional Cloud Backup. Because these send live updates between phones, they need you signed in and online. Anyone watching your live link in a browser needs neither — they just tap your link.

Note: Map street tiles load from the map provider when you have data, so the background map may look blank in zero-signal areas — your GPS trail and stats are still recorded with full accuracy.

Questions, answered simply

Everything you need to know before heading out.

Quick answers in plain language. For account-specific help, sign in and use the feedback form in your account area.

What works without internet or an account?

All of your personal tracking does. You can record car drives, motorcycle rides, walks, bike rides, and runs with zero cell signal and no account — your route, speed, distance, duration, and elevation are calculated right on your phone and saved there. Hands-free Auto-Stop & Auto-Start, history charts, GPX import/export, and shareable route cards also work completely offline.

How does 1-Tap Live Location Sharing work, and does the viewer need the app?

Tap Share Live Location right from the Home screen (or during any active trip). Anyone you send the link to can watch your live path, speed, direction, and arrival countdown in any web browser with no app and no account required — or open it inside the TrackMe app if they have it installed. When you pause or stop at a light, viewers see a clean paused indicator instead of messy GPS zig-zags, and TrackMe automatically spaces out updates (15s default) to save your battery.

What are Trusted Contacts, and do you read my phone’s address book?

Never — TrackMe never asks for your phone’s contacts permission. Instead, you can invite up to 5 family members or close friends using a private one-time link (requires TrackMe v1.9.3 or newer on both phones). Once connected, turning on Live Share can notify your Trusted Contacts automatically with an instant push alert so you don’t have to copy and paste links before driving.

How does hands-free Auto-Stop & Auto-Start (Continuous Trip Tracking) work?

If you often forget to press Stop when you get home or forget to start recording your return trip, turn on Continuous Trip Tracking in Settings → Advanced Settings. When you stay indoors for your chosen window (5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes), TrackMe automatically ends the trip and trims out the idle time you spent sitting inside. It then waits quietly in low-power standby (up to 2 hours) and automatically starts recording your next trip when you start moving again.

Who can see my live-share link, and when does it stop?

Only people who have your unique link (or Trusted Contacts you chose to notify) can view your live session while it is active. You can stop sharing in one tap at any time, and every live session automatically expires when your chosen timer ends.

Can I export my trips or delete my data anytime?

Yes. You can export any single trip as a standard GPX file from the app, request a full ZIP archive of your cloud profile and ride history from the Account section below, or permanently delete your account and every cloud record in one step.
